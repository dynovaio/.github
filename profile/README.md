## 🌌 About Us

The name **Dynova** is born from the fusion of constant dynamism and the energy of a *nova* (a new star). In this GitLab space, we open our doors to the tech community.

We apply pragmatic intelligence to create Open Source tools and enterprise solutions under three principles:

* 🟣 **Disruptive Innovation:** We break traditional models and challenge established norms to generate new opportunities and solve complex problems.
* 🟢 **Dynamic Execution:** We adapt quickly, creating secure, scalable code with a clear focus on performing concrete actions without losing sight of business objectives.
* 🔵 **Transparency and Trust:** We deliver value with empathy, transparency, and honesty. That's why we share our knowledge, building solid and reliable foundations for the community.
